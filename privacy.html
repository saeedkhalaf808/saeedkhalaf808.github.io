Overload privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overload does not collect, store or share any personal information. We do not ask you to create an account.

Your high score, daily challenge result and streak are saved only on your own device, using your device's local storage. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they are removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

If you choose to sign in with your email address so you can use your account on another device, we store that email address and your progress (name, level, XP, streak and best scores) in our database. We use the email only to sign you in and never share it. You can ask us to delete it at any time using the contact below.

If you play online, your chosen name and scores are stored in our database and shown on the public leaderboard.

If you turn on reminders, Overload asks your device for permission to show notifications. The reminder messages are created on your device from your own scores and streak.

Your chosen name is checked against our database so that no two players share a name.

Overload does not currently show ads, use analytics or contain third-party tracking. If that changes in a future version, this policy will be updated before the change is released.

Overload is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.
